Privacy Policy

Rocket Sketch — Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rocket Sketch ("the app"), developed by DS DEVELOPER, handles information. We built Rocket Sketch to work fully offline, and we take a simple approach to your privacy: we don't collect, store, or transmit any personal data from you at all.

In short: Rocket Sketch does not require an account, does not connect to the internet to send data anywhere, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. The app does not require sign-in, and no data about you or your gameplay is ever sent to us or to any third party.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

Rocket Sketch saves a small amount of game data directly on your device using standard browser/app local storage, so your progress is remembered between sessions. This includes things like:

  • Best distance reached and coins collected
  • Number of games played and crash count
  • Sound on/off preference
  • Whether you've completed the in-game tutorial

This information never leaves your device — it is not uploaded, synced, or shared with us or anyone else. You can clear it at any time using the "Delete My Data" option inside the app's Settings, or by clearing the app's storage/data from your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Rocket Sketch does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tools, or social features. No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected in the first place.

4. Children's Privacy

Rocket Sketch does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app has no accounts, no data collection, and no ads, it does not present the privacy risks associated with apps that do collect user data.

5. Permissions

The app requests no special device permissions (such as camera, microphone, location, or contacts) beyond what is needed to run as a standard offline game.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that involves data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
