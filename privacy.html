Privacy Policy — Rocket Sketch

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Rocket Sketch ("the App") is developed by DS DEVELOPER ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how, in plain language.

1. Information We Collect

Rocket Sketch does not require an account, login, or registration, and we do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.) on our own servers.

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard browser/WebView local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us:

  • Best distance, best coin count, best time, and number of games played
  • Your in-game coin and diamond balances
  • Which ships (Rocket, Helicopter, Jet), colors, backgrounds/templates you have unlocked and selected
  • Sound, music, and vibration on/off preferences
  • Whether you have completed the in-game tutorial

Uninstalling the App or clearing your browser/app data will permanently erase this locally stored information.

2. Advertising

Rocket Sketch shows interstitial advertisements using Google AdMob. Ads may appear at points such as when you change your rocket, background template, or graphic colors.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information to deliver and measure ads, which can include your advertising ID, device information, general location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction data. This processing is governed by Google's own policies, not by us directly:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads & AdMob Consent Policy
During development and testing, the App may display Google's official test ad units instead of live ads. This has no effect on the data-handling described above once live ads are enabled.

3. In-App Currency

Coins and diamonds are earned only through gameplay (for example, collecting items or defeating obstacles). Rocket Sketch does not currently offer real-money purchases; nothing in the App requires a credit card, payment account, or app-store billing.

4. Permissions

  • Internet access — required to load and display advertisements.
  • Vibration — used for optional haptic feedback (e.g. on crash), controlled by the in-game Vibration setting.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

5. Children's Privacy

Rocket Sketch is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If this App is configured or marketed specifically toward children, ad settings will be configured to request Google's child-directed treatment and non-personalized ads, in line with Google Play Families Policy and applicable laws such as COPPA.

6. Data Sharing

Other than the advertising processing described in Section 2, we do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
